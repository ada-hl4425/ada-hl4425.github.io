A / INVERSE PROBLEMS

RE-BAE v1.0

Reliability-aware sparse urban emission inversion

A Bayesian approximation-error method that constructs structured representativeness-error covariance from fine-minus-coarse wind differences. Evaluated with real winds over Los Angeles and Dallas–Fort Worth.

Bayesian inferenceCalibrationNetwork design

THE QUESTION
Can adding monitors make us more certain without making us more accurate?

When correlated error was ignored, nominal 90% interval coverage fell below 20% in the evaluated settings.

B / ENVIRONMENTAL EVIDENCE

Rainfall & PM₂.₅ in Singapore

A five-year matched-control study

My Imperial MSc independent research project separates rainfall-associated PM₂.₅ reductions from normal temporal change across Singapore’s five reporting regions. Rain events were matched to rain-free controls on timing, duration, and pre-rain pollution.

Supervisors: Dr Yves Plancherel · Dr Fangxin Fang

Matched controlsTime seriesRainfall
Explore the thesis repository ↗
MEAN EXCESS REDUCTION
2.49 µg/m³

95% CI: 2.00–2.98 · 1,436 matched events

Individual-event prediction intervals crossed zero. The evidence supports an average effect across events.

C / PHYSICS-GUIDED LEARNING

Urban air quality under changing weather

Physics-regularized neural modelling in London

An inverse modelling workflow for emission-source estimation from sparse surface observations. Physical consistency is embedded in learning, with sensitivity analysis used to examine the robustness of source estimates.
